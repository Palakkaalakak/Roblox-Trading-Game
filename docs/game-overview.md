# Game overview (paste-ready context)

A Roblox trading game modelled off of trading in Roblox (eg Roblox limiteds) but rendered accessible to all players and expanded with other features and the main proposition of being able to earn real Robux. Currently, players get a starter item bundle at the beginning of the game (drawn from set limited starter pools so as not to inflate certain items) generated asymmetrically (eg some players have very few high value items whereas others have many low value items) to create asymmetrical demand, revealed through a starter takeover screen, after which a short tutorial walks the player to the Crafter NPC and has them complete one guaranteed Unique Craft before releasing them into the game (the tutorial currently ends there; where it sends the player next is still undecided). Tied with this, you have the Crafter NPC, where multiple items can be crafted together in recipes to generate a higher-value item, or one high-value item sacrificed for many low-value items. There are Unique Crafts that are unique to every player and help with asymmetrical demand. There is also a rolling feature where you can either use gems or items to roll every X minutes (X tbd), and a gem store NPC which offers a rotation of items every y minutes at different prices and discounts. There is a trade board where players can post trade ads, a market tab where players can list items for gems, and a bulk buy tab where people can buy 1 item in bulk where multiple players can chip in for their bulk buy order. Players can also trade directly one on one with each other, and there is a quest and rank system (Noob, Casual, Regular, Trader) that unlocks these features progressively as players complete quests, rather than gating currency itself, along with daily and weekly quests and a long ladder of profit titles for players who finish the rank chain, with a player's current rank or profit title shown floating above their character in-world. There is also a crew feature similar to Clans in mobile games, with group goals and perks, there are trade booths, AFK pads for idle rewards, and leaderboards. Once reaching a set threshold (for now a placeholder of 100,000 gems) players can cash them out at a set exchange rate (placeholder of 100 gems for 1 robux rn). Gems are the currency of the economy gotten only through direct robux purchase or by watching ads at a rate of 10 gems per 1 robux that reaches the dev (to ensure solvency), and beyond gem packs there are other Robux products like subscriptions, dev products, and gamepasses (eg trade ad boosts, extra booth slots, a quest skip). Gems CANNOT be distributed for free or in any way that does not ensure we get an equivalent robux per 10 gems given.
